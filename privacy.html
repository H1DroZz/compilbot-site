Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026.

CompilBot is used only by its author. It does not collect, sell or share personal data of any other person.

TikTok data. When the author authorizes CompilBot, TikTok issues access and refresh tokens and basic profile information (open ID, scopes granted). These tokens are stored only on the author's own server and are used only to upload the author's videos to his TikTok inbox as drafts. They are never shared with third parties.

Deletion. Access can be revoked at any time from the TikTok app (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions); the stored tokens are then deleted.

No cookies, analytics or tracking are used on this website.
