CompilBot

CompilBot is a personal tool that builds highlight compilations of a football player's actions from full match videos, then sends them to the owner's TikTok account as drafts. The owner reviews each video in the TikTok app, writes the caption and decides whether to post it.

CompilBot is used only by its author, for his own TikTok account. It is not offered to other users.
